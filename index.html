Pipeline

Live source → manifest → clean chunks → cited instruction rows.

Bounded live collection

Collects from Laws of Bangladesh, BEPZA/EPZ policy sources, and procurement-related sources when reachable, with retrieval timestamps and provenance.

Business-first scope

Covers company setup, contracts, customer policies, labor, EPZ/BEPZA, foreign investment, partnerships, JVs, and expansion pathways.

Source-task filtering

Rejects major mismatches, such as using payment-instrument excerpts for company setup, refund policy, or disciplinary timeline answers.

Repair notebooks

Colab notebooks add persistence checks, Drive backups, Hub checkpoints, and smoke probes before accepting a trained adapter.

Replicate it

A practical route for copying the process or adapting it to another business domain.

01

Collect live sources

Run the collector with bounded source counts, preserve URLs, timestamps, source types, and failure logs.

02

Build cited rows

Generate JSON-oriented SFT examples with source limits, citations, missing facts, refusal behavior, and expert handoff language.

03

Train with persistence

Use Colab notebooks with pre-run persistence checks, Google Drive checkpoints, and Hugging Face adapter checkpoints.

04

Benchmark before release

Check JSON validity, citation presence, refusal behavior, source relevance, company setup grounding, and fallback usage.

Safety posture

Business exploration support, never legal advice.

The useful part is the restraint.

The assistant is designed to cite supplied sources, refuse unsupported predictions, ask for missing facts, and separate source-supported points from broader checks that need another source or expert review.
